About LibrarAin

The next generation AI-powered library management system that revolutionizes how students interact with library resources, using facial recognition, OCR technology, and smart recommendation engines.

Our Smart Features

Face Recognition System

Our advanced facial recognition technology provides a secure and convenient validation process for issuing and renewing books, eliminating the need for physical ID cards and reducing wait times.

Optical Character Recognition

LibrarAin uses state-of-the-art OCR technology to extract student details automatically when books are borrowed, making the entire process seamless and efficient while reducing manual data entry errors.

Smart Book Recommendations

Discover new books tailored to your interests with our AI-powered recommendation system that analyzes your reading history and preferences to expand your knowledge horizons.

Automated Notifications

Never miss a return date again. Our system sends automated email notifications for due dates, overdue books, and successful transactions, helping students avoid late fees.

How LibrarAin Works

1

Registration

Students register through webcam verification. The system captures facial data and scans ID cards using EasyOCR to extract email, faculty, and name information.

2

Book Borrowing

Students are verified via facial recognition. Book details including author, title, and ISBN are captured and stored in the system with automatic checkout.

3

Book Return

The validation process confirms student identity and book condition. The return is recorded and the book returns to circulation.

4

Smart Notifications

Students receive email notifications for due dates, successful returns, and personalized book recommendations based on their reading history.

Technologies We Use

Computer Vision

Utilizing advanced computer vision algorithms for accurate facial recognition and authentication at every checkpoint.

EasyOCR

A powerful optical character recognition tool that enables our system to extract text data from ID cards and book details with high accuracy.

Machine Learning

Our recommendation engine uses collaborative filtering and content-based approaches to suggest books that match student interests.

By leveraging cutting-edge AI technologies, we're not just managing books - we're fostering a community of knowledge sharing and discovery.
